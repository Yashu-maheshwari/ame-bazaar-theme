# Meesho Template Comparison Report

## Field Comparison
The official Meesho templates are highly category-specific. While standard pricing and compliance fields remain identical across all categories, product-specific attributes and measurement requirements change drastically.

### Key Variances
1. **T-Shirts:** Requires general Fabric, Color, Neck, and exact Chest/Length/Shoulder sizes.
2. **Frocks/Dresses:** Requires Bust Size, Waist Size, and often Kids Weight for children variants.
3. **Clothing Sets:** Splits attributes strictly into Top Fabric vs Bottom Fabric, and Top Chest Size vs Bottom Waist Size.
4. **Jeans/Bottomwear:** Requires highly specific bottomwear metrics like Inseam_Length, Rise_Length, Hip Size, Thigh Size, Stretch, and Distress.

## Sourcing & Automation
*   **WooCommerce:** SKU, Inventory, Description, Product Name.
*   **Raintech POS:** MRP, Selling Price (Meesho Price).
*   **Business Profile:** Manufacturer Name/Address/Pincode, Packer Name/Address/Pincode, Country of Origin.
*   **Derivable:** Generic Name, Net Quantity.
*   **Human Input (Batchable):** Colors, Fabrics, Fits, Patterns, and standard Measurement Profiles.

## Estimated Realistic Automation
*   **T-shirts:** ~40%
*   **Jeans:** ~35%
*   **Clothing Sets:** ~30%
*   **Frocks:** ~40%
